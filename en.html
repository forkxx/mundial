    01What if the goal is not reached by the deadline?

    I will run a Brazilian Warcraft 3 championship with 100% of the amount raised. Your support still helps national Warcraft 3.

    02Why is the deadline a month before the event?

    To leave time to buy tickets and book lodging without inflated prices.

    03What if money is left after the trip is covered?

    I will also run a championship with the remaining amount raised by the community.

    04Where can I follow everything?

    YouTube, Kick and Twitch at /forkxx, plus TikTok and Instagram.

    05Where does the 15% of live PIX donations go?

    It helps cover the channel costs until the event. Only the remaining 85% of those donations counts toward the R$ 11,000 goal. With direct PIX and PayPal, 100% goes to the goal.
